Here are answers to the questions businesses usually ask before choosing a plan.

No. You provide your business information and requirements. We handle the website and technical implementation.

It includes a custom business website, free default web URL, responsive design, business information, WhatsApp contact integration and 5 free edits per year after project delivery.

What is the difference between Growth and Xpert?

Growth adds CRM, customized WhatsApp messaging and transaction tracking. Xpert adds a premium website, advanced CRM, Gmail automation and advanced transaction tracking.

Is the website mobile friendly?

Yes. All plans are designed to work across mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Are domain and third-party costs included forever?

The stated project scope is included. Domain renewals, paid APIs, premium hosting, software subscriptions and other third-party costs may be separate where applicable.

Can you build something not listed in the plans?

Yes. Tell us what you need. We can discuss a customized solution and quote based on the required features.
