Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about X ELECTRONICES.

What services does X ELECTRONICES provide?

We provide digital assistance for government-related services, payments, document services, travel bookings, recharges and other available digital services.

Who can use your services?

Our services are intended for eligible customers in West Bengal, subject to the rules, eligibility and requirements of the relevant service or authority.

Can I check my previous transactions online?

Yes. Use the My Transactions option on the website and enter your registered mobile number to access the transaction records available for your account.

How can I contact X ELECTRONICES?

You can contact us through WhatsApp or phone at 91637 54511. You can also use the WhatsApp buttons available throughout the website.

Are government service approvals guaranteed?

No. X ELECTRONICES provides digital assistance. Final approval, eligibility, processing time and decisions remain with the relevant government authority or service provider.

Where is X ELECTRONICES located?

X ELECTRONICES is based in Liluah, Howrah, West Bengal and provides digital assistance for eligible people across West Bengal.
