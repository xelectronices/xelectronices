Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

X ELECTRONICES respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you contact us, use our digital assistance services, or use the customer transaction portal at xelectronices.in.

Depending on the service you request, we may receive information such as your name, mobile number, email address, address, service-related details, transaction details, and documents or information that you voluntarily provide for completing a requested service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only as reasonably necessary to provide requested digital assistance, communicate with customers, maintain service and transaction records, respond to support requests, and comply with applicable requirements.

3. Transaction Portal

The customer transaction portal may use a registered mobile number to locate transaction records from our transaction data source. The portal is provided for customer convenience. A mobile-number lookup should not be considered password-level authentication.

Some services may involve official government portals, payment platforms, travel providers, Google services, WhatsApp, or other third-party systems. Their own privacy policies and terms may apply when you use those services.

5. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell customer information. Information may be shared with an official authority, service provider, payment platform, or other third party when it is necessary to provide a service requested by the customer or where required by applicable law.

We take reasonable practical steps to protect information handled through our services. However, no internet-based system or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Analytics

The website may use basic browser technologies or third-party tools where necessary for functionality, performance, or understanding website usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Customer Choices

You may contact X ELECTRONICES to ask about information associated with your service records or to request correction of inaccurate information, subject to applicable requirements and our ability to verify the request.
